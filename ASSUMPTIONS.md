# MVP assumptions

- The production repository and business rules remain untouched; the prototype follows the prompt pack's isolated status and visual model.
- `docs/brand/` does not exist in the canonical repository. Brand assets are copied from `frontend/public/`, and existing production WOFF2 Lato files are copied from `frontend/src/assets/fonts/`.
- The prototype uses deterministic fictional data and no network/API calls.
- Unconfirmed policy items are represented as `TO CONFIRM` or preview banners rather than invented approvals.
